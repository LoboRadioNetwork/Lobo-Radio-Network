LOBO TELEVISION

Four channels, on the air around the clock. Channel 1 is the classic Lobo TV — westerns and cartoons with a word from our sponsors. Channel 2 is our Spanish channel — classic films in Spanish, all day. Channel 3 is all Bible, all day — The Living Christ, the Sermons from Science, and the Hymn Sing. Channel 4 is the Lobo Sci-Fi Network — forgotten science-fiction classics and drive-in double features, all day. Turn the set on and work the TUNER on the side of the set… or pop open the DVR and pick a classic yourself.
